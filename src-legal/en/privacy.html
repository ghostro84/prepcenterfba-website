2. Collection and storage of personal data and the nature and purpose of its use

a) When visiting the website (server log files)

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. – see section 4). When our website is accessed, the browser used on your device automatically transmits information to the servers of our host, where it is stored in so-called log files. The following information is recorded without any action on your part:

  • IP address of the requesting computer
  • date and time of access
  • name and URL of the file retrieved
  • website from which access is made (referrer URL)
  • browser used and, where applicable, the operating system of your computer as well as the name of your access provider

The data referred to above is processed by us for the following purposes:

  • ensuring a smooth connection to the website
  • ensuring convenient use of our website
  • evaluating system security and stability, and for further administrative purposes

The legal basis for the data processing is Art. 6(1) sentence 1(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest follows from the purposes for data collection listed above. Under no circumstances do we use the data collected for the purpose of drawing conclusions about your person.

Storage period: We ourselves have no access to these log files and do not evaluate them. Storage takes place on the systems of our host GitHub, Inc.; according to its own information, access data including IP addresses is stored for a maximum of 30 days and thereafter deleted or aggregated. Longer storage takes place only where this is necessary in order to investigate a specific security incident or case of misuse; in that event the data records concerned are deleted as soon as the incident has been conclusively clarified.

b) Contact form

You can send us an enquiry using the contact form on this website. The details you enter are transmitted: name, e-mail address, service requested and message text.

Transmission is effected technically via the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which receives the form data and forwards it as an e-mail to our address. The service is operated by a provider outside the European Union and, according to the information on its own website, is attributable to the provider Devro LABS; complete, verified provider details (legal form and registered office) are not currently available to us. The data transmitted comprises your name, your e-mail address, the service selected, your message text and your IP address. Processing takes place on servers outside the European Economic Area (in particular in the USA).

The legal basis for processing your enquiry is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of pre-contractual measures). We base the transfer to a third country exclusively on your explicit consent pursuant to Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR, which you grant by ticking the consent checkbox and submitting the form. You may withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR); the lawfulness of the processing carried out up to the withdrawal remains unaffected.

Notice of the risks of this third-country transfer (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR):
  • There is no adequacy decision of the European Commission pursuant to Art. 45 GDPR in respect of the recipient.
  • There are no appropriate safeguards within the meaning of Art. 46 GDPR (in particular no standard contractual clauses available to us) and no binding corporate rules.
  • Authorities of the recipient state may access the data under the law applicable there without your obtaining knowledge of this or being afforded effective legal protection.
  • Your data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure) may not be enforceable against the recipient.

If you wish to avoid this, please write to us directly by e-mail at b2b@prepcenterfba.eu instead. Use of the contact form is not necessary in order to contact us; you will suffer no disadvantage by using e-mail.

We are working to replace this form service with a solution hosted in the European Union and will amend this privacy policy once the changeover has taken place.

Your enquiry is processed by us in order to deal with it and in case of follow-up questions. Enquiries that do not lead to the conclusion of a contract are deleted by us no later than six months after the enquiry has been finally dealt with. Where a contract is concluded, the retention periods under commercial and tax law apply (depending on the type of document, generally six, eight or ten years pursuant to § 257 HGB (German Commercial Code) and § 147 AO (German Fiscal Code)).

3. No cookies – exclusively local storage (localStorage)

This website sets no cookies – neither technically necessary cookies nor marketing, tracking or analytics cookies. No web analytics and no tracking take place; no third-party script is loaded when the pages are accessed. For that reason there is also no cookie banner and no consent request on this website.

The only browser technology used is localStorage (local storage) for two technically necessary entries. These entries remain on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party. Because access to this information is strictly necessary in order to provide the service expressly requested by you, no consent is required for it under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). The legal basis for the subsequent processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a functioning website displayed in the desired language).

No data is transmitted to third parties via local storage; with regard to the service providers used by us (form service, hosting) please see sections 2 b and 4.

No automated decision-making (Art. 13(2)(f) GDPR): Automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22(1) and (4) GDPR does not take place. We do not use any procedures that take decisions about you without human involvement, and we do not create user profiles.

9. Right to object

Right to object pursuant to Art. 21 GDPR

You have the right, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to object at any time to the processing of personal data concerning you which is carried out on the basis of Art. 6(1) sentence 1(f) GDPR (legitimate interests). If you object, we shall no longer process your personal data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Where your data is processed for the purposes of direct marketing, you have the right to object at any time without giving reasons; the data will thereafter no longer be processed for those purposes.

The objection is not subject to any particular form and may be sent informally to: b2b@prepcenterfba.eu.
