Our prep services in detail

Every service is performed in line with Amazon guidelines and billed transparently. No flat packages, no surcharges.

Goods receipt

Goods receipt inspection and booking in

We take in your consignments on working days at fixed receiving times – whether a single parcel, a carton or a pallet. Every delivery is counted immediately, checked externally and booked into our warehouse system. You receive the goods receipt confirmation by e-mail.

  • Receipt of parcels, cartons and Euro pallets
  • Counting and documentation on arrival
  • Photos in the event of damage or discrepancies
  • Immediate notification of any discrepancies
  • Booked in with a status update by e-mail on the day of arrival

Quality control

Inspection to your specification

We check your goods against an individually agreed inspection protocol – from a simple visual inspection to a detailed functional check. The result: an inspection report with photos, which we send you by e-mail.

  • Sample check or 100 % inspection, as you prefer
  • Checks for: quantity, packaging, damage, colour deviation
  • Individual checklist to suit your product requirements
  • Photo documentation with a time stamp
  • A clear inspection report (passed / defects) by e-mail

FNSKU labelling

Precise labelling to the Amazon standard

Incorrect or missing FNSKU labels are one of the most common causes of problems with FBA inbound shipments. We print and apply your FNSKU labels precisely – with a quality check on every single label.

  • Label PDF from your Seller Central – or printed by us from your FNSKU list
  • Applied to the product or the outer packaging
  • Old or incorrect barcodes covered on request
  • Scan validation: every label is checked after application
  • Compatible with all Amazon labelling requirements

Packaging & prep

Packaging protection in line with Amazon guidelines

Amazon sets clear packaging requirements, depending on the product category. We apply the correct method and make sure your products arrive safely and are booked in without objection.

  • Polybag (transparent) to Amazon's minimum requirement
  • Bubble wrap for shock-sensitive products
  • Foam inserts for glass, electronics and ceramics
  • Repacking into plain or branded cartons
  • Suffocation warning stickers on polybags (mandatory for Amazon)

Bundling & multipacks

Assembling sets and multipacks properly

Bundles and multipacks are an effective way to raise the average order value and set yourself apart from competitors on a listing. We assemble your sets to your bill of materials.

  • Assembly to your bill of materials (SKU/FNSKU)
  • Application of the overall bundle label
  • Packing as a set in a carton or polybag
  • Multipacks: 2-packs, 3-packs, 6-packs – larger sets on request
  • We maintain your bills of materials – coordination and changes by e-mail

FBA inbound shipment

Direct inbound shipments to Amazon FBA

You create the shipping plan in your Seller Central – we have no access to your Amazon account. You send us the shipment ID and the shipping labels; we make up the consignment, label the cartons to Amazon's specification and hand them over to the carrier.

  • Consignment made up against your shipment ID from Seller Central
  • Carton labelling and packing list creation
  • Palletising to Amazon's pallet specification
  • Handover to the carrier and tracking passed on to you
  • Delivery to every German warehouse assigned by Amazon
