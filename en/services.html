Receiving

Goods Receipt Inspection & Booking

We accept your shipments on business days at fixed receiving times – whether individual package, carton or pallet. Every delivery is counted immediately, inspected externally and booked into your customer portal.

  • Receipt of packages, cartons and Euro pallets
  • Counting and documentation on receipt
  • Photos for damages or discrepancies
  • Immediate notification of any differences
  • Booking and status update in the portal on the day of receipt

  • Spot-check or 100% inspection selectable

FNSKU Labeling

Precise Labeling to Amazon Standard

Incorrect or missing FNSKU labels are one of the most common causes of FBA delivery problems. We print and apply your FNSKU labels precisely – with quality control of every single label.

  • Printing from your Amazon Seller Central (CSV import or manual)
  • Application to product or outer packaging
  • Covering of old or incorrect barcodes on request
  • Scan validation: every label is checked after application
  • Compatible with all Amazon labelling requirements

Packaging & Prep

Packaging Protection to Amazon Guidelines

Amazon has clear packaging requirements – depending on product category. We apply the correct method and ensure your products arrive safely and are booked in without objection.

  • Poly bag (transparent) to Amazon minimum requirement
  • Bubble wrap for shock-sensitive products
  • Foam inserts for glass, electronics, ceramics
  • Repackaging into neutral or branded cartons
  • Suffocation warning stickers on poly bags (Amazon requirement)

Bundling & Multipacks

Professionally Assemble Sets and Multipacks

Bundles and multipacks are an effective way to increase average order value and distance competitors on listings. We assemble your sets to your bill of materials.

  • Assembly to your bill of materials (SKU/FNSKU)
  • Application of the overall bundle label
  • Packaging as set in carton or poly bag
  • Multipacks: 2-pack, 3-pack, 6-pack – larger sets on request
  • Bill of materials management in the customer portal

FBA Delivery

Direct Delivery to Amazon FBA

We create the shipment plan in your Seller Central, label the shipping cartons to Amazon's requirements and hand the shipment over to the carrier. You only need to approve the shipment.

  • Creation of the Amazon shipment plan in your Seller Central
  • Carton labelling and packing list creation
  • Palletisation to Amazon pallet specification
  • Carrier handover and tracking forwarded to you
  • Delivery to all Amazon-assigned German warehouses
