Basics

What is an FNSKU?

The FNSKU (Fulfillment Network Stock Keeping Unit) is the identifier Amazon uses to assign a product unit to a particular seller. It differs from the ASIN and the EAN: while the ASIN and the EAN describe the product, the FNSKU links a physical unit to your seller account.

That is precisely why it is indispensable in an FBA warehouse. Without an FNSKU label your goods end up in what is known as commingled inventory and are mixed with other sellers' stock – with the risk that your customers receive another seller's goods and that you have to answer for their condition.

Requirements

What Amazon requires of the label

The requirements are specific and are checked by machine at goods receipt.

  • The barcode must be fully scannable – no creases, no curvature, no reflective film over it.
  • Existing foreign barcodes (EAN, UPC, manufacturer codes) must be completely covered.
  • The label must not be stuck across edges, openings or closures.
  • On polybagged goods the label belongs on the outside of the bag, where it is visible.
  • On sets and multipacks the outer selling unit carries the label, not the individual components.
  • Labels must be produced by thermal or laser printing – inkjet smudges and is rejected.
Process

How we work

You provide us with the labels as a PDF from Seller Central, or you send us the FNSKU and the quantity and we print the labels here. In both cases we check before application that the FNSKU, ASIN and quantity match the consignment you announced.

Each unit is then labelled individually: foreign barcodes are completely covered, the label is placed on a smooth, flat surface and finally checked with a scanner on a sample basis.

If we notice that a batch does not match the announcement – a different quantity, a different variant, damaged packaging – we report it before doing any further work.

Sources of error

The most common labelling mistakes

By far the most common mistake is a manufacturer's barcode that has not been completely covered. The scanner at the fulfilment centre then reads the wrong code and the unit is assigned to the wrong stock.

Almost as common are labels on curved surfaces or on the edge of a carton: the barcode cannot be scanned in one pass, the unit has to be reworked manually and Amazon charges a fee for that.

The third classic is the wrong FNSKU from an earlier batch – for example when a variant has changed but the same set of labels has been carried over. That is why we run a check before every order instead of applying labels unchecked.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I supply the labels myself?
Yes. You can send us the PDF file from Seller Central and we will print the labels and apply them. Alternatively, you send us only the FNSKU and the quantity and we create the labels.
What does covering an existing barcode cost?
That is already included in the price of €0.39 per unit. There is no separate surcharge.
Do you also label polybagged goods and bundles?
Yes. On polybagged goods the label is applied to the outside of the bag so that it is visible; on bundles it goes on the outer selling unit, with a corresponding bundle notice.
How quickly can this be done?
For standard orders, labelling takes place within 24–48 hours of goods receipt.
