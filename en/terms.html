In the event of contradictions between this Annex and the remaining provisions of these GTC, the provisions of this Annex shall prevail in respect of the processing of personal data on behalf of a controller. Liability is governed by Art. 82 GDPR and the statutory provisions; § 8 of these GTC remains unaffected as regards the remaining contractual claims.
