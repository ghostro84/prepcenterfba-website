Delivering goods

Please give us advance notice of cartons and pallets by e-mail – with the number of packages, the expected delivery date and the carrier. Label every package on the outside with your company name. That allows us to schedule receiving and book your goods in without delay.

For clients based in another EU country we invoice under the reverse-charge procedure, which requires your VAT identification number. Without a VAT ID we will of course still send you a quote.

Alternatively, you can write to us directly at b2b@prepcenterfba.eu at any time.
