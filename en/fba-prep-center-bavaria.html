Bavaria location

FBA prep center in Bavaria – our Freilassing site

In south-eastern Bavaria, right on the border with Salzburg: short routes for sellers in southern Germany, Austria and northern Italy.

Location

Why the location works for sellers in southern Germany

Freilassing lies in the Berchtesgadener Land district, immediately on the Bavarian–Austrian border and therefore on the axis between Munich, Salzburg and the Alpine crossing towards Italy. For sellers in Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, Austria and northern Italy the journey to us is short – and so is the journey from us to the Amazon sites in southern Germany.

For you that means lower delivery transport costs and fewer days in transit before your goods are available in the FBA network.

We naturally also work for sellers elsewhere in Germany and throughout Europe – delivery is then made by freight forwarder or parcel service.

Services

Services at the site

At the Freilassing site we provide the full range of services: goods receipt for cartons and pallets, quality control, FNSKU labelling, polybagging and bubble wrap packaging, bundling, interim storage, and making up and handing over FBA consignments to the carrier.

You can also have returns and removal orders sent to our address in Bavaria.

Please give us advance notice of deliveries by e-mail so that we can schedule receiving.

Catchment area

The regions we typically work for

The majority of our deliveries come from Bavaria – in particular from the greater Munich area, from Rosenheim, Traunstein and the Berchtesgadener Land – plus Salzburg and Upper Austria, as well as European sellers who need a point of entry into Germany.

We deliberately operate this one site only. Pages promising prep services for cities where no warehouse exists help nobody – the goods have to be transported to wherever they are processed in any case.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I bring the goods in myself?
Yes, by prior appointment arranged by e-mail. Please contact us in advance so that receiving can be scheduled.
Do you accept freight deliveries on pallets?
Yes. Pallet receiving costs €6.00 per pallet and covers unloading, a unit count and booking in. Please give us advance notice of freight deliveries.
Do you also work for sellers based in Austria?
Yes. Because we are immediately on the border with Salzburg, the journey is particularly short for Austrian sellers.

Our services are aimed exclusively at businesses within the meaning of § 14 BGB (German Civil Code).
