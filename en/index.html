Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does processing take after goods receipt?
For standard orders up to 500 units, we process your goods within 24–48 hours of receipt. We discuss larger orders individually with you.
What is the minimum order quantity?
There is no minimum unit quantity, but a minimum order value applies per delivery. The exact amount is stated in our price list.
Do you also work with non-German Amazon accounts?
Yes. We work with sellers from all over the world. Communication in English is no problem. A prerequisite is an active Amazon seller account for the European market.
